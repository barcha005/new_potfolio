05 — Questions

Asked, and answered plainly

Will the domain be registered in my name?+

Yes — always. The domain is registered in your name and the website is your property. You can move it to any provider at any time; nothing is held back.

How does payment work?+

50% in advance when work begins, and the remaining 50% only after your website is live and you have seen it working. We accept JazzCash, EasyPaisa and bank transfer.

How long does a website take?+

Basic takes 3–5 days and Standard 5–7 days, counted from the day your photographs and details reach us. If we ever expect a delay, you will hear it from us first.

Can I change things after launch?+

Yes. Two rounds of minor changes — a phone number, a photograph, a paragraph of text — are free after launch. Larger changes are always quoted before any work starts.

Will my website rank first on Google?+

Every site includes a proper basic SEO setup, which is what allows it to be found on Google at all. But rankings grow over weeks and months, and any provider who guarantees you the first position is not being honest with you. We would rather be honest.
