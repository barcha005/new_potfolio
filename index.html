FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

 

Will the domain be registered in my name?

Yes, always. The domain is registered in your own name and the website is your property, not ours — you are free to move it at any time.

How does payment work?

50% in advance when the work begins, and the remaining 50% after your website goes live. We accept JazzCash, EasyPaisa and bank transfer.

How long will my website take?

The Basic package takes 3–5 days and the Standard package 5–7 days, provided your photographs and details reach us on time.

Can I request changes after launch?

Yes. Two rounds of minor changes (a number, a photograph or text) are free after launch. For larger changes, we will always quote the price in advance.

Will my website rank first on Google?

Every website includes a basic SEO setup that helps it appear on Google, but rankings take time — and no honest provider can guarantee a first-place ranking. We will never promise you one.
